subject: Brand Identity & Website

description: AV-X is an AV company from Toronto, Canada. They needed a new logo, brand, and website created to make them look more reputable and help them stand out from their competitors. A unique part of their business is that they offer AV as a Service to their clients. This end-to-end solution and support was the key idea behind the drawing of the logo. The 'AV' creates two arrows showing the secure and long term service AV-X delivers. The new brand and website has now helped them become a more modern looking and easily recognizable company setting then apart in their industry.
